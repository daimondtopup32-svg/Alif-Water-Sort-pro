Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Alif Water Sort Pro ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android game application (Package: Alif.WaterSortpro) collects, uses, and safeguards your information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The app collects minimal information strictly necessary to provide puzzle gameplay and cloud synchronization:

  • Account & Profile Data: If you register or play as a guest, we store a username, avatar emoji, coins earned, and current level unlocked.
  • Game Progress: Completed puzzle levels, stars earned, and game preferences (sound/vibration/language) stored locally using Room Database and synchronized via Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic logs, device model, and advertising IDs (Google AdMob) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our application integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core application support and security. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads to grant in-game tubes and bonuses. AdMob Policies
  • Firebase Realtime Database & Firebase Analytics: Used for secure cloud synchronization of levels and leaderboards. Firebase Privacy

3. How We Use Information

Your data is used solely for:

  • Saving and restoring your puzzle levels and achievements.
  • Displaying global leaderboard rankings.
  • Providing rewarded video ads for extra empty tubes and game tools.
  • Ensuring app stability and fixing crashes.

We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are hashed using industry-standard SHA-256 before local or cloud storage. All network communications with Firebase are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our puzzle game is safe for family and children. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it promptly.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may request deletion of your account and game progress at any time by contacting our support email with your username.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date.
