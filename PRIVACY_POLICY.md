## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Alif Water Sort Pro** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Profile & Account Data:** When you create an account or play as a guest, we store your chosen username, avatar emoji, coins earned, and current level progress.
- **Game Progress:** Stored locally in an encrypted Room SQLite database and synchronized via Firebase Realtime Database.
- **Diagnostics & Ads:** Anonymous device model and advertising identifier (Google AdMob) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our application integrates trusted third-party SDKs:
- **Google Play Services:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google AdMob:** [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
- **Firebase Realtime Database:** [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 4. How We Use Data
- To track puzzle levels, save unlocked themes, and calculate scores.
- To display global leaderboard rankings.
- To provide rewarded video ads for extra empty tubes and game tools.
- **We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.**

## 5. Security & Passwords
Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 before storage. Communications with Firebase use secure HTTPS/TLS encryption.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our puzzle game is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
