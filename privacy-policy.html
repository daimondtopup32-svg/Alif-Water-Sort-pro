Privacy Policy

Application: Alif Water Sort Pro
Package Name: alif.WaterSortpro
Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Alif Water Sort Pro! Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through our casual puzzle game.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver and improve our gameplay features:

  • Game Progress & Local State: Level numbers reached, puzzle completion moves, collected game coins, and selected visual skins. This is stored securely on your device.
  • Account Information: If you register an account, your username and encrypted password hash are saved. We never store plain text passwords.
  • Crash Data & Basic Diagnostics: Standard non-identifying diagnostics provided by Android to ensure the game runs smoothly without errors.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

This game uses trusted third-party SDKs to provide analytics, leaderboards, and in-game rewards:

  • Google AdMob (Google Play Services): Displays banners, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded video ads (to unlock additional empty tubes). AdMob may use device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Realtime Database & Analytics: Used to sync high scores and leaderboard rankings for multiplayer competition.

3. Children's Privacy

Alif Water Sort Pro is designed for general audiences and puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

4. User Rights & Data / Account Deletion Request

You have full control over your data. Users can request complete deletion of their account, leaderboard entries, and game progress at any time.

How to request deletion:

  • Send an email to our support team at daimondtopup32@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • Include your username / Player ID.
  • All associated game records, statistics, and leaderboard data will be permanently wiped from our Firebase servers within 7 business days.
